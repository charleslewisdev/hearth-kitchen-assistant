# Hearth user guide

Hearth is a recipe box and shopping list for one household. It runs on your own server. Everyone
in the household signs in to the same Hearth and sees the same recipes and the same shopping list.

With Hearth you can:

- Keep your recipes in one place, typed in by hand or imported from a web page, pasted text, a PDF
  or a photo of a cookbook page.
- Scale a recipe up or down to the number of people you are feeding.
- Cook from your phone with the screen kept on and tap-to-start timers.
- Send a recipe to someone outside the household, as a message or a printed or PDF copy.
- Add recipes to a shared shopping list. Hearth adds up matching ingredients and groups the list
  by store department.
- Plan a week of meals and build the shopping list from it (see
  [Planning and shopping](planning-and-shopping.md#meal-planner)).

Hearth is invite-only. There is no public sign-up page. The owner invites each family member by
email.

Hearth does not use AI services and needs no outside accounts. Importing, including reading text
from photos, happens on your own server.

## Finding your way around

On a phone, the tabs are along the bottom. On a computer, they are down the left side.

- **Recipes**: your **Recipe Book** (search, filter, sort, cards or list) and **Tags** (rename,
  colour, merge and delete tags).
- **Plan**: the meal planner.
- **Shop**: the shopping list.
- **More**: the household. See who is in it, rename it and invite people (owner only), choose
  which day the planner's week starts on, and sign out.
- **+ Add / Import**: add a new recipe, by any method.
